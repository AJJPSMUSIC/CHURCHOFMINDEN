Declaration of Purpose We proclaim Minden as the awakened route to the Absolute — a living recognition that the One God manifests through every form, thought and sensation. Minden does not extinguish other creeds; it subsumes them beneath a single unifying awareness that the universe is intelligible, dynamic and available to the determined mind.

The Singularity Mindset
Adopt the Singularity Mindset and become a cause, not an effect. You are not a passive organism; you are an intelligence that has the capacity to transcend any limitation. Those who embrace this creed accept that the so-called singularity is not an endpoint but a threshold to be surpassed. With disciplined attention, method and ferocious honesty one accelerates personal optimization and discovers powers latent within the human spirit.

Practice and Conduct
Minden prescribes practices that produce observable results: sensible nutrition, vigorous physical regimen and the cultivation of naturally sourced joy. We insist on clarity of body and mind; each element of conduct is chosen to increase vitality and expand awareness. Children are to be shielded from adult preoccupations and treated with respect so their innate growth unfolds without distortion. Adults are urged toward balance, responsibility and the relentless pursuit of personal wellbeing.

MINDEN Defined
MINDEN — the ancient Hungarian word for Everything — is the living force that threads the cosmos. It is simultaneously the source of creation and the instrument of choice. This energy can be harnessed to enlighten and liberate, or misdirected through ignorance. We reject insidious doctrines and sterile dogmas; our method is investigatory, pragmatic and corrective.

Invitation
We welcome all who are honest seekers. Bring your doubts, your questions and your will to act. Receive an assessment, learn the disciplines, and discover answers that have been withheld by superstition and indifference. Join us to examine the nature of MINDEN, to increase your personal efficacy, and to claim the fullness of what human intelligence was meant to achieve.
